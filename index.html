Who we are

INT TY CO., LIMITED (國際天逸有限公司) was founded in August 2020. We source products in China and export to Southeast Asia, Europe, the US and Korea — running a multi-market, multi-batch operation built on supplier screening, quality control and reliable delivery.

We are not a factory. We are the middle hub that de-risks the supply chain for high-value goods — where a single QC miss or shipping error is too expensive to ignore.
